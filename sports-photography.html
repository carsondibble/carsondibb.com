UTAH PHOTOGRAPHY

Sports Photography in Utah

I’m Carson Dibb, a Utah photographer who documents school and local sports—from the pace of live play to the people and moments around the game. My portfolio includes football, basketball, and lacrosse.

Action, athletes, and the story of the game

Sports move quickly, so I look for the decisive moments: a pass in the air, a shot at the basket, or the expression that makes a play memorable. I’ve photographed games from field level as well as from the stands, adapting to the access and conditions available at each event.

Tell me the sport, date, venue, and what you need photographed. I’ll confirm availability and discuss the best coverage for your event.
