UTAH PHOTOGRAPHY

Concert Photography in Utah

I’m Carson Dibb, a Utah photographer drawn to live music, expressive performers, dramatic stage lighting, and the connection between artists and their audience. My concert portfolio includes indie rock and live music events.

Live music, from the stage to the crowd

Concerts are fast-moving, low-light environments. I work with the available light and changing action to capture performance moments as well as the atmosphere in the room. Coverage can focus on the artist, the crowd, or the overall feel of the event.

Get in touch with the artist or event name, venue, date, and any access details so we can discuss whether the coverage is a fit.
