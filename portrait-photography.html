Portrait Photography in Utah

I’m Carson Dibb, a Utah photographer creating portraits and creative photoshoots in outdoor and urban settings. My portfolio includes individual portraits, graduates, and portraits made at events.

A relaxed, collaborative photoshoot

Whether you have a location and concept in mind or are still figuring out the look, we can talk through the setting, timing, and style before the shoot. I aim to make portraits that feel natural to the person and the moment rather than overly staged.

For availability, send the type of portrait you’re looking for, your preferred date, and the Utah location you have in mind.
